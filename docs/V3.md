# Bible Arena v3

v3 is a new, fast web game. It is served by the same Express server at `/`, `/d/:code` and `/v3/*`. The classic Expo app (v2) keeps its own routes: `/play`, `/quiz`, `/room`, `/ai-battle`, `/friends`, `/leaderboards`, `/profile` and so on.

## What's in it
- **Modes:** Fill in the Gap, Who Am I (4 clues), Bible or Myth, Arena Quiz, Mixed, Sudden Death and the Daily Challenge.
- **Levels:** Believer (the classic v2 questions), Scholar and Theologian.
- **Progress:** ranks from Seeker to Apostle, streaks, 24 achievements, and sound and vibration toggles.
- **Pastor duels:** `POST /api/v3/duels` creates a set and a link at `/d/CODE`, with a WhatsApp/OG preview.
  - The challenger plays the same questions. Both players see each other's progress live (polling).
  - The result screen compares the two players question by question, then offers a rematch.
- **Leaderboards:** weekly, all-time, Church League, rivals (head-to-head), and today's daily.
- **Church League (v3.4.0, `server/v3/churches.ts`):** a church, fellowship or campus group creates a team (name 3–50, city, state; moderation: no links/phone numbers/obscenities, duplicate name+city blocked, 2 creates per player per day, 6 per IP per hour). Members join with a 6-character invite code or the link `/c/CODE` (WhatsApp/OG preview, `og-church.jpg`). One team per player; the team lead can remove members; an empty team closes; a lead who leaves hands over to the longest-serving member.
  - Points = round scores (`bible_arena.rounds.score`) a member earns after joining. Weekly league = Monday 00:00 Lagos time to the next Monday; all-time = since joining. Last week's top church is recorded once as champion (🏆 badge + title count).
  - API: `GET /api/v3/league?period=week|all`, `GET /api/v3/churches/mine`, `GET /api/v3/churches/:id`, `GET /api/v3/churches/code/:code`, `POST /api/v3/churches` (create), `POST /api/v3/churches/join {code}`, `POST /api/v3/churches/leave`, `POST /api/v3/churches/:id/remove {member}`, `GET /api/v3/churches/meta`.
  - Storage: SQLite tables `v3_churches`, `v3_church_members`, `v3_church_wins`, `v3_church_creates` (daily create limit log, 2 days kept) (created by the module; an empty team is deleted outright; snapshotted to Postgres with the rest of the SQLite file, plus an immediate snapshot on every church write). Account deletion and guest→Google merge carry/remove the membership. A member's `players.church` is the team name.

- **Live Multiplayer Social (v3.5.0, `server/v3/social.ts`, client `web-v3/social.js`):** open, on-demand multiplayer (no scheduled shows).
  - Presence: `POST /api/v3/social/hb {pid, busy, mute, live}` every ~25 s while the app is open, visible and touched in the last 5 min; online = seen in the last 75 s. In memory only (no DB work per heartbeat). Returns the online count, the Live now list (when asked) and at most one pop-up.
  - Live now (`GET /api/v3/social/live`): open challenges, quick-match duels being played (anyone can join the same questions at `/d/CODE`) and Live rooms whose host turned on "List in Live now" (`public`, late join up to 200).
  - Quick Match: `POST /api/v3/social/match` pairs with the longest-waiting open challenge (the waiter becomes the duel creator) or opens one; `GET /api/v3/social/match/:id` (poll 2.5 s; a wait not polled for 25 s ends), `POST …/:id/join`, `POST …/:id/cancel`. After 60 s the client offers keep waiting / Arena Bot (`POST /api/v3/social/bot`: a duel with a finished "Arena Bot 🤖" entry, right ~75/62/50% by level) / solo.
  - Challenge the winner: `match {duel: CODE}` (server finds the winner; the winner gets a targeted pop-up/push; a winner opening it gets "winner stays on"), `match {room: CODE, lt: token}` from a Live room final.
  - Pop-ups: max 1 per person per 2 min (a challenge aimed at you is not held back), never mid-round, mute in Me → Settings.
  - Web Push (`server/v3/webpush.ts`, VAPID + aes128gcm on node:crypto): `GET /api/v3/push/key`, `POST /api/v3/push/subscribe|unsubscribe|test`, `GET /api/v3/push/status`. Sent when an open challenge is still waiting after 15 s, to subscribed players not in the app; quiet hours 22:00–07:00 Lagos, max 3 a day and 1 an hour per device; dead subscriptions (404/410) are deleted. Keys: env `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, `VAPID_SUBJECT` (Render env secrets only). Subscriptions: SQLite `v3_push_subs` (deleted with the account). iPhone: only when the site is added to the Home Screen (iOS 16.4+).
- **Verified churches (v3.5.0, `server/v3/churches.ts` → registerVerify):** the team lead requests (`GET|POST /api/v3/churches/:id/verify`: name, role, the church's official public page (required), optional official email/phone) and gets a code from the church name (`BA-LFCA-7K3`) to post on the official page. The owner reviews at `/admin/verify` (key in env `BA_ADMIN_KEY`, sent as `x-admin-key`; 10 wrong tries per 15 min per IP) and approves, rejects (reason shown to the lead) or revokes. Never automatic. The tick is cosmetic; a rename drops it until reviewed again. 1 open request per church, 3 requests a day per account. SQLite `v3_church_verify`, `v3_church_verified`.
- **Speed (v3.5.0):** Postgres pool 10 (env `BA_PG_POOL`, 2–15), shared leaderboards and church standings cached 20 s (a church write clears the standings), a round save is 2 queries instead of 4.

## Data
- **Durable data** lives in Postgres: Supabase, schema `bible_arena`, role `bible_arena_app` (least privilege), env `BA_DATABASE_URL`.
- **Legacy SQLite** (users, friends, rooms, v2 challenges) is restored from `bible_arena.sqlite_snapshots` at boot. It is snapshotted back every 15 s when it changes, and again on shutdown (`server/persist.ts`). Render's disk wipes no longer reset accounts.
- **Question bank:** `web-v3/bank.json` is built from `files/bible-arena/v3-content` (KJV-checked). The server re-scores every answer against it.

## Test
`node scripts/v3-smoke.mjs <base-url>` runs the two-player duel, round and leaderboard checks end to end.
